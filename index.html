Cap rate follows the standard definition: net operating income (rent, minus vacancy, capital improvements, management, tax, insurance, and HOA) over the home price, with no mortgage in the equation. Cash-on-cash return is your actual return on the money you put in — it subtracts your mortgage payment, any extra payment, and PMI, then divides by your down payment plus closing costs. Both are estimates for planning purposes, not a substitute for a professional underwriting review.
